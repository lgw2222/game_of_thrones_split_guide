and Braavos

Compare the real filming spots with scenes from Game of Thrones, then tap the map to go there. Start in Split’s old town; the last three stops need a ride.

Best quick visit: Palace cellars and Papalićeva Street are close together in the old town. If she has time for one outing, choose Klis Fortress for the recognizable Meereen walls and the view. The show added sets and visual effects, so angles will differ today.

Easy order for a day

Walk the palace cellars → Papalićeva Street. With a car or tour, continue to Klis Fortress; add Žrnovnica and Kaštilac if she has more time. From Split, these are separate outings rather than part of the old town walking loop.
